# Credits and licences for the artwork in `pacifer/assets`

Everything here is free to use, but the LPC art **requires the artists to be
credited** in the game (a credits screen or an included file). Ship this file
with the game.

## LPC Revised (`assets/lpc-revised/`)

- Source: https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC
- Licence: OGA-BY 3.0 (based on CC-BY 3.0; commercial use allowed, including on
  mobile, as long as credit is given).
- Compiled by Eliza Wyatt (DeathsDarling). Artists: Eliza Wyatt (DeathsDarling),
  Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Stephen Challener (Redshrike), Johannes Sjölund (Wulax),
  BlueCarrot16, BenCreating, Durrani, YuriNikolai, Hyptosis, Craftpix.net 2D Game Assets.
- Per-file detail is in the `Credits.txt` files copied next to each sprite sheet,
  including the pieces used for the castle (stone walls, arched doors, ornamental
  windows, pillars), the paving, the indoor furniture and the wall torches.
- The castle itself — curtain walls, crenellations, towers, gatehouse and keep —
  is not a stock sprite. It is composed tile by tile from those sheets by
  `tools/castle.py`.

## Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator (`assets/ulpc/`)

- Source: https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator
- Every piece carries its own licence; the exact list (authors, licences, links)
  for the pieces actually used is in `assets/ulpc/CREDITS.csv`.
- Licences found among the pieces used: OGA-BY 3.0, CC-BY-SA 3.0, GPL 3.0,
  GPL 2.0, CC0. Many pieces are offered under several licences at once, in which
  case you may pick one.
  **Careful:** pieces available *only* under CC-BY-SA or GPL require derivative
  work to be published under the same licence. Check this before a commercial
  release, or swap those pieces for OGA-BY / CC0 ones.
- Authors of the pieces used: Benjamin K. Smith (BenCreating), Carlo Enrico Victoria
